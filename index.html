What is this event

A day built for sharp minds and sharper arguments.

If you love a good argument, enjoy challenging complex ideas, or want to test your ability to think under pressure, the Inkspire Debatathon is the perfect opportunity. This event brings together sharp debate, critical analysis, and persuasive communication. Participants will face off in rounds that bridge logic, philosophy, ethics, and contemporary culture — a fantastic platform to refine public speaking, practice rapid problem-solving, and develop strong leadership capabilities.

The event will culminate in the Literary Court — a bonus courtroom-style simulation that places iconic fictional characters and ethical conflicts on trial. Participants will assume the roles of prosecutor and defense attorney to debate complex ideas in a competitive setting, with the opportunity to compete for top prizes and awards.

FAQs

Still have questions?

Do I need a team, or can I participate individually?+
Register as a team of 3, or with 2 — or even completely on your own. We'll pair you up with other participants to complete your team.
Do I need prior debate experience?+
No prior debating experience needed — beginners are welcome.
Will I know the topic in advance, or is it impromptu?+
Topics are given on the spot, followed by 10 minutes of preparation time.
What happens if I lose early — am I completely out?+
No. Losing a round doesn't eliminate you from the day. You'll get another opportunity to compete in the Honourable Mention Round against strong teams from later stages, with a chance at winning the Honourable Mention.
Can I take part in both the debate and Literary Court?+
Participants can volunteer to take part in Literary Court — selection is based on your individual score in the debates.
How will I know which classroom to go to for each round?+
You'll be handed a list on the day of the event with your classroom/room allocation for each round.
Will I get feedback on my performance from judges?+
Yes — you can ask your judges for feedback.
Can parents/guardians attend to watch?+
No, it is a student event only.
What happens if my partner drops out?+
We can assign you another partner, or you can continue alone — whichever you prefer.
